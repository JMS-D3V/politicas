2. What the app does

The app helps on cattle handling day at the chute: open a session (weighing, vaccine, dewormer, medication, weaning, check), identify each animal by tag (typed or read by an RFID wand in keyboard mode), record weight and products, show average daily gain (ADG), arrobas and withdrawal periods on the spot, and export CSV and PDF. It is not a herd management system and does not replace official traceability or animal transit systems.

It works offline, with no account and no JMS server. Data stays on your device (see the Privacy Policy).

3. Calculations are informational — read carefully

  • ADG, arrobas (live and carcass), carcass yield, the “weight off the curve” alert and withdrawal dates are estimates based on the data you entered and on defaults you can change. They will be wrong if the input is wrong.
  • The withdrawal period is the one you typed from the label. The app has no drug database and does not check it. It uses the safe side (slaughter released only the day after the period ends), but it does not replace the product label or the advice of the responsible veterinarian.
  • The app must never be the only basis for deciding slaughter, sale of animals or sale of milk. Check the label, the veterinarian and applicable sanitary rules first.
  • Weights and arroba prices vary with fasting, scale, region and market; the app’s values are not a certified report.
