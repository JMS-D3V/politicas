Privacy Policy

Peso da Lida · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains in plain language what data the Peso da Lida app (“app”) handles, where it lives, who it is shared with and how you exercise your rights. It follows Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law 13,709/2018) and the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Law 12,965/2014).

In case of conflict between translations, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Controller

  • Controller: JMS — Gestão e Soluções Automatizadas (“JMS”, “we”).
  • Privacy contact and data subject requests: suporte@jmsgestao.com.br

2. In one sentence

The app works with no account, no login and no JMS server: your herd and everything you type stay on your device. JMS does not receive or see that data. The network is used only for ads (Google AdMob, free plan), for buying the plan (Google Play) and, if you choose, for backups to your own Google Drive.

4. Retention

  • On the device: until you delete it (Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all data on this device), clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall.
  • Android automatic backup: follows Google’s rules; you can turn it off or delete it in device and account settings.
  • Google Drive: the app keeps the 5 most recent backups and deletes older ones. You can remove everything in Google Drive on the web (Settings → Manage apps → Peso da Lida → Delete hidden app data) and revoke the app’s access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access). “Disconnect” in the app deletes the stored email.
  • Files you exported or shared: stay where you put them; the app cannot delete them.
  • Google (AdMob and Play): retention set by Google (policies.google.com/privacy).

5. Sharing

JMS does not sell or share your herd data. The only third parties are Google services:

  • Google AdMob (ads, free plan only). According to Google’s documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad and app interactions, and diagnostics and performance data, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. The paid plan removes ads.
  • Google Play (purchase). Payment is processed by Google Play. The app never sees card or billing details.
  • Google Drive (optional). Backups go to your account, in a folder only this app can see. The app requests a single scope, drive.appdata: it cannot list, read or change any other file. JMS has no access to that folder.
  • Android automatic backup. Done by the operating system in your Google account.

International transfer (LGPD art. 33): Google services may store and process data outside Brazil under Google’s contractual safeguards and privacy policy.

You have the right to confirmation, access, correction, anonymization/blocking/deletion, portability, deletion of consent-based data, information on sharing, information on refusing consent, and withdrawal of consent. Since nearly everything stays on your device, you exercise most of these inside the app:

7. Security

Data lives in the app’s internal storage, protected by Android’s sandbox. Connections to Google use HTTPS. The app does not log tokens, backup content or what you type. A backup file holds your whole herd: keep it safe.

8. Children

The app is a work tool for cattle handling, not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect data from children or teenagers. Contact us at suporte@jmsgestao.com.br if you believe a minor sent us data.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the date above when the app starts handling data differently, and announce relevant changes on the Google Play listing and, when possible, in the app.
